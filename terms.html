1. What Is Kazi Eats?

Kazi Eats is a mobile-based food delivery platform that connects customers with local township restaurants, food vendors, and independent delivery drivers.

Kazi Eats does not prepare, cook, or package food. Kazi Eats provides the technology and platform that enables food ordering and delivery coordination between customers, restaurants, and delivery drivers.

3. Placing Orders

An order becomes binding between you and the restaurant once the restaurant accepts the order, subject to these Terms and any applicable consumer protection laws.

4. Delivery Rules

Due to informal or changing addressing systems in some township areas, customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery information.

Customers may be required to provide a GPS location, physical address, landmarks, directions, or other information that helps the delivery driver locate the delivery address.

Customers must be available at the delivery location to receive their order.

Delivery Waiting Period

Drivers may wait for a maximum of 10 minutes after arriving at the designated delivery location.

If the customer cannot be reached or fails to collect the order within the applicable waiting period, the order may be cancelled or otherwise handled in accordance with the circumstances of the order.

Where permitted by applicable law and the circumstances of the cancellation, an electronic payment may not be refundable. A future-order penalty of R20 may also apply where such a charge has been clearly communicated to the customer.

Respect for Drivers

Kazi Eats delivery drivers are members of the communities in which they operate and must always be treated with courtesy and respect.

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or abusive behaviour towards drivers may result in account suspension or termination.
