Restaurant FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to join KaziEats?

Our current restaurant packages start at R80 per month for Basic and R150 per month for Premium.

What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

Basic provides access to the KaziEats restaurant platform and core restaurant services. Premium includes additional marketing, visibility, featured opportunities and promotional exposure.

Do I get a separate restaurant app?

Yes. Once your restaurant application is approved, KaziEats provides access to a separate restaurant application.

What happens after I submit my application?

Our team reviews the information you provide and contacts you regarding approval, package activation and the next steps.

Can I upgrade from Basic to Premium?

Yes. You can contact the KaziEats team if you would like to discuss upgrading your restaurant package.
