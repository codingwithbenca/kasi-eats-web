FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Open KaziEats, browse available restaurants, add your favourite items to your cart and complete checkout.

How can I pay?

You can use supported online payment methods or Cash on Delivery where available.

Can restaurants join KaziEats?

Yes. Restaurants can apply through our restaurant partner registration process.

Can I become a delivery driver?

Yes. Drivers can register, complete the required verification process and begin delivering when approved.
