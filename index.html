About KaziEats

Our Mission

KaziEats was created to connect customers with local restaurants while creating opportunities for independent delivery drivers. Our goal is to make food delivery affordable, reliable and accessible for every community.

We believe local businesses deserve modern technology that helps them grow while giving customers a simple and secure ordering experience.
