Driver FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply to become a KaziEats driver?

Complete the application form on this page and continue to WhatsApp. Your application information will already be prepared in the WhatsApp message.

Does KaziEats provide a driver application?

Approved drivers receive access to the KaziEats driver platform for managing deliveries.

What vehicle can I use?

You can indicate your vehicle type during the application process. Available vehicle options may depend on the delivery requirements in your operating area.

Can I apply if I have no delivery experience?

Yes. The application allows you to indicate that you have no previous delivery experience. Your application will still be reviewed by the KaziEats team.

What happens after I apply?

The KaziEats team reviews your information and contacts you regarding the next steps in the driver onboarding process.
