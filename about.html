Supporting Local Restaurants. Empowering Communities.

Who We Are

KasiEats is a South African food delivery platform built to connect customers, restaurants and delivery partners through reliable technology and secure online payments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower local businesses, create earning opportunities for delivery drivers, and provide customers with a simple, affordable and reliable food ordering experience.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading community-focused food delivery platform by helping local businesses thrive through innovation and excellent service.
